# Personal Alpha Metrics

## North Star

不使用单一“赚钱分数”。

核心看板分四组。

## A. Discovery Quality

- Material Events Detected
- Qualified Opportunities
- False Positive Rate
- Missed Material Event Rate
- Evidence Failure Rate

## B. Execution Quality

- WATCH → READY conversion
- READY → ENTER conversion
- Entry slippage
- Invalidated Before Entry %
- Trades Avoided by Risk Gate
- Trades Avoided by Extended Reaction

## C. Outcome Quality

- Net P&L
- Gross P&L
- Expectancy / trade
- Win rate
- Avg win / avg loss
- Profit factor
- Max drawdown
- Sharpe-like risk-adjusted metric（仅作辅助）
- Average holding days
- MFE / MAE

## D. Personal Edge

按 Opportunity Family 统计：
- trades
- expectancy
- profit factor
- max drawdown
- average MFE
- average MAE
- invalidation rate
- sample size confidence

## Rule

当 family 样本：
- < 10：不得得出稳定结论
- 10–29：标记 LOW_SAMPLE
- 30–99：可做 provisional calibration
- >=100：可进入正式 rule review
